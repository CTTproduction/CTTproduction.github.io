PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy explains how CTT Production ("CTT Production," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our mobile games, browser games, websites, and related services (collectively, the "Services"). CTT Production is an independent game studio based in India.

Please read this Policy before using the Services. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Services. A particular game or platform may provide an additional notice where its data practices differ from this general Policy.

1. SCOPE

This Policy applies to the games and other Services published by CTT Production, including the titles linked from https://cttproduction.github.io/. It does not govern third-party websites, app stores, advertisements, or services that we do not control.

2. INFORMATION THAT MAY BE COLLECTED

Depending on the game, device, region, privacy choices, and features you use, we and our service providers or advertising partners may process:

• Device and technical information: device type and model, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen settings, app version, mobile carrier, browser type, network connection type, and similar technical information.

• Identifiers: IP address, advertising identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, app-instance identifiers, device or vendor identifiers, and identifiers assigned by an advertising, analytics, or platform provider. Availability of these identifiers depends on your device settings, permissions, and applicable law.

• Approximate location: a general location, such as country, region, or city, inferred from an IP address or device settings. We do not intentionally request precise GPS location unless a specific feature clearly asks for it and the required permission is obtained.

• Gameplay and usage data: game progress, session time, levels, in-game actions and events, feature interactions, settings, ad impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad completion, purchases or purchase status, and referring or exit information.

• Diagnostics and security data: crash reports, error logs, performance data, timestamps, and information used to detect fraud, invalid traffic, abuse, or security incidents.

• Information you provide: information included in an email, support request, review, survey, or other communication with us, such as your email address and message. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

• Purchase information: an app store or payment provider may process your payment details. We generally receive transaction status, product purchased, country, and a transaction or receipt identifier, rather than your full payment-card details.

Some data is collected automatically by SDKs integrated into the Services. CTT Production may receive only aggregated reports or limited event-level information from those providers; the provider may separately process additional information under its own privacy policy.

We may use information to:

• select, deliver, measure, limit, and report advertisements;

4. ADVERTISING AND ADMOB MEDIATION

Our Services are generally free and may be supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob as an advertising platform and mediator. Mediation allows an ad request to be offered to multiple advertising sources so that an eligible source can provide an ad. As a result, Google or a third-party mediated ad network may receive an ad request and process information from your device even when that network does not ultimately display the ad.

The advertising information may include IP address, advertising or app identifiers, approximate location, device and app information, consent signals, ad request details, ad impressions and interactions, and fraud-prevention data. Advertising partners may use this information to deliver ads, measure performance, attribute installs or actions, cap ad frequency, detect invalid activity, and, where you have consented or the law otherwise permits, personalize ads or build advertising profiles.

Our currently integrated or available advertising and mediation technologies include:

• Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK — our primary ad platform and mediator. Google may serve its own demand and route ad requests to other ad sources.
  Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  How Google uses information from sites or apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  Ad controls: https://myadcenter.google.com/

• Liftoff Monetize (formerly Vungle) — a mediated advertising network and SDK adapter that may deliver, personalize, and measure ads and help prevent advertising fraud.
  Privacy Policy: https://liftoff.ai/privacy-policy/
  Opt-out information: https://liftoff.ai/opt-out/

• Unity Ads / Unity LevelPlay technologies — a mediated advertising provider and SDK adapter that may deliver and measure ads, perform attribution, and detect fraud.
  Game Player and App User Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  Privacy choices: https://unity.com/legal/do-not-sell-my-personal-information

• Other mediated ad sources and ad-technology providers — we may add, remove, or enable additional adapters or bidding partners through AdMob. The provider selected can vary by game, country, device, ad format, availability, and mediation configuration. Google describes AdMob mediation and maintains information about eligible ad sources and ad-technology partners here:
  AdMob mediation: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/13411971
  Ad technology partners: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903

An SDK being present or available does not necessarily mean it receives data on every ad request. Each provider controls its own processing under its privacy policy. The current consent message shown in a game, where applicable, provides the most relevant vendor and purpose information for that user and region.

6. PERSONALIZED ADS AND YOUR CHOICES

Our apps use a GDPR consent flow for users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other applicable regions. Where required, we or our partners ask for consent before using information for personalized advertising or related storage and access. If you decline, you may still receive ads, but they should be contextual or non-personalized. Such ads can still use information such as the game being used, approximate location, device type, and fraud-prevention signals. Declining personalized ads does not necessarily reduce the number of ads.

Depending on your game and region, you may be able to review or change your choice from an in-game "Privacy," "Privacy Settings," "Manage Consent," or similar control. You can also:

• reset or delete your device's advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, in Android device settings;
• manage Google advertising preferences at https://myadcenter.google.com/;
• use the privacy or opt-out tools offered by Liftoff, Unity, and other providers; and
• contact us using the details below.

The exact device-setting names vary by device and operating-system version. Resetting an identifier or reinstalling a game may require you to make your privacy choice again.
